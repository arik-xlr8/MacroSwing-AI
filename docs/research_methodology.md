# Research methodology

**Research question.** Does integrating macroeconomic regime analysis and sector rotation improve the historical risk-adjusted performance of swing trading strategies in BIST 100 stocks?

## Four required studies

| Scenario | Question and comparison | Evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| Sector catch-up | Within a strong sector, do relative laggards catch up after accounting for technical stabilization, macro sensitivity and financial deterioration? Compare with sector median/index and peers over multiple lookbacks. | Subsequent relative returns at 5/10/20 trading sessions, hit rate, median catch-up and false positives by sector and period. |
| Calendar patterns | Do weekday and multi-session sequences persist beyond the discovery period, after costs? Compare conditional outcomes to unconditional or random baselines across index/sector direction and macro regimes. | Occurrences, conditional mean/median, out-of-sample stability, costs and multiple-testing exposure. |
| Technical reversals | Do historical band, RSI, Supertrend, KAMA, cloud and support/resistance events recur under comparable conditions? Compare single and combined conditions with both successes and failures. | Counts, bounce/failure rates, average/median strength and forward distributions at 1/3/5/10 sessions, split by direction and volatility. |
| Quarterly financial reaction | Do disclosed changes in earnings, liquidity, profitability and leverage relate to later prices? Compare strong fundamentals with weak technicals and the reverse. | Repeated post-disclosure reactions, sector-adjusted and XU100 abnormal returns. Retain the example's 1/5/20-session windows. |

## MacroSwing hypotheses

Study global and Turkish monetary tightening/easing, inflation acceleration/deceleration, employment, unemployment, expansion/contraction, industrial production, currency changes and domestic/global risk separately. Industrial production and labor indicators are proposed extensions to the minimum macro set. The sign of a rate move alone cannot determine a regime. Estimate sector sensitivities historically and permit an uncertain classification when evidence conflicts.

The proposed lens spans 3–12 months for macro trends, 5–60 sessions for swings and 1–10 sessions for technical entry timing. A supplementary 120-session outcome could test slower macro effects. These scales add research questions while preserving the assignment horizons. Apply the conceptual sequence of global context, domestic context, regimes, sectors, fundamentals, technicals and swing candidates, then verification, evidence-based explanation and human review. Use the required state order for actual orchestration.

Compare A with B for incremental sector value, C with D for macro value, and D with E for contextual value. Evaluate each on common periods, data availability, execution assumptions, risk constraints and metrics. In separate studies, test calendar and reversal signals independently. Allow zero or negative incremental value.

## Historical verification

Before examining outcomes, specify hypotheses, eligibility, features, outcome windows, confirmation timing, common coverage, costs, slippage, execution and risk rules. Separate discovery from validation/test or use rolling walk-forward analysis. Preserve the decision-time vintage; release after close and confirmation requiring later bars both affect the earliest eligible signal. Shift positions and compute forward outcomes only after signal formation.

Compare with buy-and-hold, XU100 and a sector benchmark where available. Report cumulative return, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, win rate, benchmark difference, number of signals and regime-specific results. Report underpowered samples, missing coverage, failed signals and instability explicitly. Financial performance and harness accuracy/reproducibility measure different outcomes.

## Universe limitations

The 30-stock 2026 roster includes 24 simplified sectors. Eighteen have one member. Automotive, Basic Metals / Steel, Electric Utilities, Retail Trade, Telecommunications and Transportation / Airlines have two each. A singleton has no internal comparison peer after excluding the subject. Even a two-member sector has just one other stock. Require a dated, documented external basket/benchmark or report insufficient evidence. Keep the frozen population, dated index membership and external comparison histories separately identifiable. Discuss survivorship and coverage bias when studying periods before roster selection.
